# Optional preset: remote EMEA startup roles

Copy only the parts that match your preferences into your private `data/profile.md`. Complete location, eligibility, experience, compensation, and exclusions yourself; this preset is not a candidate profile.

- Search product-oriented engineering roles at startups hiring remotely within Europe or EMEA.
- Example role families: product engineer, frontend engineer, full-stack product engineer, forward-deployed engineer, applied-AI engineer. Keep only the families supported by your experience and interests.
- Evaluate real scope: product ownership, UX, customer interaction, stack, balance of frontend/backend work, and required specialist knowledge. Titles alone are not sufficient.
- Verify that the employer can hire in your actual country, and distinguish employee hiring from contractor arrangements.
- Verify remote restrictions and travel expectations on the live employer page. EMEA eligibility is not the same as worldwide remote work.
- Capture published compensation and equity separately. Do not substitute an estimated market range for an employer's published pay.
- Use Interested and Applied as positive examples and Uninterested notes as negative feedback. Preserve explicit exclusions and all current stages.
- Save only verified net-new matches at the top of Prospects; a run with no new strong match is a valid result.
